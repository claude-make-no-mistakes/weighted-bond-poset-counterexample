# X draft

An 18-vertex star refutes Conjecture 7.3 of González D’León and Wachs. For the star with 17 leaves, the coefficient of the Schur function indexed by (15,1) in f₈² − f₇f₉ is −354,585,660. It stays negative for every larger star.

The proof extracts just two coefficients after keeping two variables. It also gives a degree-three failure for stars with at least 78 leaves. [Link to the proof]
